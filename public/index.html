Real WhatsApp / Instagram / Facebook webhooks route through a debounce buffer first (default 6s) so that a burst of rapid messages from the same person — e.g. "hii" then "how are you" a second later — is combined into one turn and answered once, instead of firing two separate AI replies.

Voice call demo

Browser mic + speech synthesis standing in for a real Twilio phone call, same Gemini pipeline underneath.

🎙️
Click the orb and speak. Realty AI will reply out loud, just like an incoming phone call handled by the voice channel (Twilio webhook in production).
Call transcript will appear here.

Uses your browser's Web Speech API (Chrome recommended) purely for this local demo's mic/speaker — no credentials needed to try it. The production voice channel (src/channels/voice.js) instead answers real phone calls via Twilio's <Gather input="speech"> / <Say> webhooks and needs Twilio credentials in .env.
